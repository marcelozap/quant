How to use this

A park for your market brain.

This is a decision-support space, not a signal vending machine. Make the ride earn its place by leaving one clear observation or question.

Walk the park: Click a district or move your avatar with arrow keys / WASD.

Visit homes: Open a symbol, write your thesis, and mark what would change your mind.

Use the news board: Capture a link or headline as context to verify, not as a trade command.

Close the loop: Write one nightly note and one song. Your history becomes useful when it has structure.
